# How to add my publications?

1. Navigate this link in a browser: [https://github.com/ARCOS-UC3M/arcos-uc3m.github.io/tree/template/generate_publications/bibs](https://github.com/ARCOS-UC3M/arcos-uc3m.github.io/tree/template/generate_publications/bibs).

2. Click on add ```Add file``` > ```Create new file```.
3. If you don't have writing permissions on the repo, you should create a fork and the propose changes through a ```Pull request```.

    3.1. Click on  ```Fork this repository```.
    
    3.2. Name your file ending with a ```.bib``` extension and paste your references in the text box.
    
    3.3. After pasting your references click on ```Commit changes...``` button. 

    3.4. Write a commit message and click on ```Propose changes```.

    3.5. On the next screen click on ```Create pull request```. 

    3.6. Add a title to the request and click on ```Create pull request```. 

    3.7. Your request will be reviewed and after approval and merge, a GitHub workflow will be launched to add your publications to the website.

## How to obtain a bibtex of my publications?

1. Go to your profile on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/).
2. Choose the publications you want to add to the website.
3. Click on ```Export > BibTex```.
