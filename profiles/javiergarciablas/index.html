About

I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M) in 2010, graduating “cum laude” with an International Mention. Following my defense, I was awarded the Extraordinary Doctorate Award. Today, I hold the position of Full Professor (Profesor Catedrático de Universidad).

My research lines focus on supercomputing architectures and large-scale storage systems, with a strong track record in technology transfer. Among other projects, my work includes COVID-19 diagnosis with CRUE-Banco Santander, heliostat plant simulation for CENER, risk simulation in financial products for BBVA, and next-generation communication mechanisms in avionics for AIRBUS. Recently, as a result of this technology transfer, I have been involved in six software licenses currently in commercial exploitation with SEDECAL, a European leader in the development of high-frequency generators and X-ray systems.

My major research lines are High-Performance Computing, Large-scale distributed systems, Data-intensive computing and storage, and IoT and real-time systems.

During my pre- and postdoctoral stages, I completed a total of four research stays at internationally renowned institutions. I have visited the HLRS in Stuttgart, Germany, the DKRZ in Hamburg, Germany, and the Argonne National Laboratory in Chicago, USA. To fund my research stays at HLRS and DKRZ, I was awarded competitive funding from the European program HPC Europa.

I have authored more than 107 publications in national and international conferences and journals, 35 of which are published in highly ranked international journals indexed in the Journal Citation Reports (JCR). Additionally, I regularly serve on the program committees of more than 8 conferences. I would also like to highlight my role as Area Editor for the International Journal of High Performance Computing Applications (Q2). Furthermore, I am an active member of the European platforms HiPEAC and ETP4HPC.

I served as the Coordinator and Principal Investigator (PI) for the European project ASPIDE (H2020 FET-HPC), which comprised partners from 6 international institutions and received €2.4 million in funding. I am also currently participating in two European projects, including ERA4TB, a public-private initiative dedicated to accelerating the development of new treatment regimens for preclinical tuberculosis, involving 31 institutions worldwide. In the past, I actively participated in five European-funded projects, namely ADMIRE (H2020), MADE (H2020), EDI@cloud (H2020), GOVeIN (H2020), Rephrase (H2020), and Repara (FP7). Additionally, I took part in the COST Action IC1305 (H2020) NESUS, where I represented Spain on the Action's Management Committee, and formed part of the scientific committee for the European project VECMA (H2020). More recently, in 2023 and 2024, I coordinated two SERAPIS European project proposals in collaboration with the SWARCO Group (one of the largest companies in Austria) and the Universität Wien.

Conferences and Keynotes

I have extensive experience organizing and leading scientific workshops and international conferences. In 2012, I was the co-organizer of the international conference IEEE ISPA 2012, held at UC3M, where I served as Poster Chair and Publicity Chair. In 2013, I co-organized the EuroMPI 2013 conference alongside Professor Jack Dongarra (recent Turing Award laureate), which stands as the premier forum for large-scale programming in computing.

In 2016, I organized and served as Program Chair for three international conferences: ICA3PP 2016, IUCC 2016, and CSS 2016. The following year, I acted as organizer and Program Chair for the international conference IEEE/ACM CCGrid 2017, ranked with a CORE A distinction. Finally, I have organized several workshops of an international nature, specifically C4BIO 2014, ESAA 2014, CCGRID Life 2015, IASDS 2015, and IASDS 2016.

Teaching

Regarding my teaching activity, I have been the coordinator of the Computer Architecture and Technology area at UC3M since 2001. Since 2000, I have coordinated the Operating Systems course in the Computer Engineering degree at UC3M, and I coordinated Real-Time Systems from 2007 to 2017.

At the Master's level, I coordinate several courses in the Master’s programs in Computer Engineering, Computer Science and Technology, the Official Master’s Degree in Analytical Methods for Massive Data: Big Data, and the Master’s in Data Science, all of which are taught in English and attended by students of multiple nationalities.

During my stay at Northwestern University in Chicago, I taught Master’s-level courses in Electrical Engineering. I have also participated in the creation of 8 courses within the UC3M OpenCourseWare program, both in Spanish and in English. I am also the lead author of the book “Sistemas Operativos: una Visión Aplicada”, currently in its third edition and widely used in Spanish and Latin American universities
