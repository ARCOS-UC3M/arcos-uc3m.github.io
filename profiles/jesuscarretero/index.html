Biography

Jesus Carretero is a Full Professor of Computer Architecture and Technology at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (Spain), where he is responsible for that knowledge area since 2000 and leader of the Computer Architecture Research Group (ARCOS).

His research activity is centered on high-performance computing systems, large-scale distributed systems, data-intensive computing, IoT, and real-time systems. He has participated in and led several national and international research projects funded by the Madrid Regional Government, the Spanish Education Ministry, and the European Union.

Prof. Carretero is currently the Coordinator of the Spanish Research Agency for the Informatics area, a European Union projects evaluator, a senior member of the IEEE Computer Society, and a member of the ACM.
