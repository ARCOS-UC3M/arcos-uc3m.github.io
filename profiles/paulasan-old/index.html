About

Paula Sánchez Checa is a second-year PhD student in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. Her research combines high-performance computing and epidemiological modelling, and she is currently working on epidemiological modelling using artificial intelligence techniques. In the field of computing, her research focuses on system modelling using AI-based tools and mathematical models, with the aim of combining the dynamic reconfiguration of resources of malleable applications and ad-hoc file systems in HPC environments. She has experience using the SLURM manager and with parallel programs using the MPI library. In the field of epidemiology, she has experience working with the EpiGraph epidemiological simulator and has collaborated with the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) by participating in the ECDC Respiratory Diseases Scenario Modelling Hub (RespiCompass), providing predictions of epidemiological and vaccination scenarios for RSV. In addition, she collaborates with Centro Nacional de Epidemiología (Spanish National Centre for Epidemiology - CNE) modelling adverse weather events and infectious diseases in Spain.
